Bible Truth Foundations

Part 3 of 3: Serving in Christ

Persecution

Lesson 5 of 16  |  Section B: Living Among Others

Opening Question

Please begin by answering this question honestly in your own words.

Have you ever softened what you believe, or stayed quiet about your faith, to avoid conflict or discomfort? What made you hold back?

Key Scripture

2 Timothy 3:12

Yes, and all who desire to live godly in Christ Jesus will suffer persecution.

Read this slowly before moving on. Notice the word "will," not "might."

Core Truth

The central idea of this lesson

Persecution is not a sign that something has gone wrong; it is the expected result of living faithfully for Christ in a world that opposed Him first.

Expected, not accidental

Jesus told His disciples plainly that He was sending them out into opposition. Persecution was anticipated, not accidental.

Matthew 10:16

Behold, I send you out as sheep in the midst of wolves. Therefore be wise as serpents and harmless as doves.

Facing opposition calls for a specific posture: wisdom and gentleness held together, neither naive nor retaliatory.

A promise, not merely a possibility

Persecution is promised, not merely possible, for all who desire to live godly in Christ Jesus. Godly living itself, not failure, is what draws this particular resistance.

2 Corinthians 11:23-24

Are they ministers of Christ? I speak as a fool, I am more: in labors more abundant, in stripes above measure, in prisons more frequently, in deaths often. From the Jews five times I received forty stripes minus one.

The real opposition is spiritual

The real opposition behind human persecution is spiritual. Believers do not wrestle against flesh and blood but against spiritual forces working through people.

Ephesians 6:12

For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age, against spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places.

Note

Recognising the deeper spiritual conflict does not excuse mistreatment or turn opponents into enemies to be despised; it reframes the response, praying and standing firm rather than only reacting personally.

Persecution spreads the Gospel

Persecution has historically scattered and spread the Gospel rather than silencing it.

Acts 8:1, 4

At that time a great persecution arose against the church which was at Jerusalem... Therefore those who were scattered went everywhere preaching the word.

Going Deeper

Galatians 6:12 adds an uncomfortable but important angle: some avoid persecution specifically by compromising the offence of the Gospel, watering down doctrine that would otherwise provoke opposition. Some absence of persecution is not peace; it is compromise.

Question My Answer Group Discussion Notes
When have you experienced any form of resistance, mockery or exclusion because of your faith? How did you respond?
What does it mean, practically, to be "wise as serpents and harmless as doves" (Matthew 10:16) when facing opposition?
How does knowing the real conflict is spiritual, not merely personal, change how you view people who oppose your faith (Ephesians 6:12)?
Galatians 6:12 suggests some avoid persecution by softening the Gospel. Where might you be tempted to do the same?

These questions are designed for open conversation at any level of experience. There are no trick questions and no single correct answer.

  1. Why do you think Jesus prepared His disciples for persecution so directly, rather than promising them ease?
  2. Share a time you saw someone’s faith grow stronger, not weaker, because of opposition.
  3. What is the danger in either extreme: being naive about opposition, or becoming bitter and retaliatory toward it?
  4. How should a believer discern the difference between suffering for righteousness and suffering as a consequence of their own poor choices?

Opposition, when it comes, is not evidence of failure. This week, practise steady faithfulness rather than retreat.

Context How I Apply This
Personal life Identify one place you have quietly avoided speaking or living out your faith to escape discomfort, and pray for boldness there.
Church community Set aside time this week to pray specifically for believers facing real persecution elsewhere in the world.
Difficult seasons If you face pushback for your faith this week, practise responding with the "wise as serpents, harmless as doves" posture rather than silence or sharpness.
Scripture to carry Memorise 2 Timothy 3:12: "Yes, and all who desire to live godly in Christ Jesus will suffer persecution." Let it remove the surprise when opposition comes.
